**Fuller idea outline (for the project page's about/idea section, if present):**
```
THE PROBLEM
Solo founders and small teams increasingly run their company treasury onchain — and the founder is a single point of failure. If they disappear (accident, illness, detention, a lost device), the bank-world answer is to FREEZE everything, and the crypto-world answer is worse: keys go silent, payroll stops, and the company dies in a week. Continuity today is a lawyer, a drawer, and hope.

THE PRODUCT
Ephor is business continuity as programmable settlement. The founder's wallet heartbeats on a schedule. Miss the window and a staged, fully cancellable succession staircase executes onchain: Stage 1, a public notice with a challenge window — nothing moves. Stage 2, a designated successor gains a CAPPED operational role (pay payroll and allowlisted vendors up to a daily limit — nothing else), while the vault's payroll reserve keeps paying the team without interruption. Stage 3, the continuity policy settles as one multi-step flow: payroll reserve funded for N periods, remaining balance split by percentages, optional currency-conversion legs, an optional cross-chain leg, residual parked in yield. Every step is evented, every step is reversible until its window closes, and the owner's live key outranks everything: one heartbeat at any stage rewinds the entire staircase. A guardian veto covers duress.

WHY ARC
Half-second deterministic blocks make succession windows precise and provable (block counts, not wall-clock trust). USDC-as-gas means a successor needs no gas token to keep the company running. App Kit Swap powers conversion legs, CCTP v2 the cross-chain leg, USYC the yield parking — and the deeper answer: self-executing continuity is impossible in banking, where disappearance means freezing. Stablecoin-native infrastructure inverts it. Conditional payments, onchain automation, and multi-step settlement aren't features here — they are the product.

BY FINALS
ContinuityVault + SuccessionPlan + guardian multisig deployed and verified on Arc testnet with invariant-tested owner-supremacy; the full staircase running unattended on compressed windows; the two demo money-shots live: the founder goes silent and payroll never misses a beat — then the founder returns, and one heartbeat rewinds everything.
```
